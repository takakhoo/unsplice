## Special rules by venue

**ESANN**
- Authors may link to online material, but it is not reviewed and not in the proceedings.
- Format is A4 with a 12.2 x 19.3 cm print area; PDF or PostScript only.
- I did not open a separate ESANN 2026 page. The site now shows 2027 guidelines, which carry the 6-page limit and esannV2.cls.
- The [special sessions list](https://www.esann.org/special-sessions) has six sessions, none on security or speech. The biomedical one reportedly mentions federated learning and privacy-preserving AI.

**Interspeech**
- The 2026 kit says the anonymity period is no longer enforced. You may post to arXiv, but the submitted PDF must carry no identifying information.
- [ISCA policy](https://isca-speech.org/Interspeech-Policy) requires "submitted to Interspeech" on any posted preprint and forbids concurrent submission elsewhere.
- Appendices must fit inside the content pages (first 4 for regular, first 8 for long).
- If generative AI was used, a "Generative AI Use Disclosure" subsection is required; it may sit on the reserved pages.
- Links in the review version must not reveal identity. Supplementary material is one anonymized ZIP of at most 100 MB with a README, which is where audio reconstructions would go.
- Format is A4, two columns, 9pt.
- I found no official GitHub repo for the kit; `github.com/Interspeech/Interspeech-paper-kit` returns 404. Overleaf is the confirmed source.
- The official 2026 author pages are now broken, so the 2026 deadline comes from the LINGUIST List posting. ISCA's page lists the 2026 dates as 28 Sep - 1 Oct.
- The 2027 site lists São Paulo but gives a University of Campinas contact address.
- 2027 special-session proposals are due 10 Nov 2026 ([dates](https://interspeech2027.org/important-dates/)).

**ICASSP 2027**
- The paper kit states a 16 Sep 2026 deadline while the important-dates page says 23 Sep 2026. Both have passed.
- arXiv preprints are allowed. AI-generated content must be disclosed in the acknowledgments. Every author needs an ORCID.
- A search snippet showed a different satellite-workshop paper deadline (25 Oct 2026) from the page I opened (11 Nov 2026). Confirm before planning around it.

**SaTML 2027**
- An "Open Science" section and anonymized artifacts were required. An "LLM usage considerations" section was required if LLMs were used.
- Prior reviews had to be appended. Presentation is in person only.

**PoPETs 2027**
- A rejected paper must skip a full issue before resubmission.
- The first page must state relevance to real-world privacy.
- Preprints are tolerated: the PC ignores outside identity information.
- The CFP references rules on AI use in writing, but I could not retrieve the text. Read [cfp27.php](https://petsymposium.org/cfp27.php) directly.
- Submission site: https://submit.petsymposium.org/

**USENIX Security '27**
- An Open Science appendix is mandatory, and artifacts are due 29 Jan 2027 for cycle 2.
- AI tools are permitted. Each author may submit at most 7 papers per cycle.

**IEEE S&P 2027**
- Generative AI use must be disclosed in HotCRP.
- A rejected paper must wait one year from its original submission date before resubmitting to S&P.

**ICLR 2027**
- The main page says "California"; the dates page says location to be announced.

## Recommendation

Open deadlines in order: S&P cycle 2 (17 Nov 2026), ESANN (18 Nov 2026), PoPETs Issue 3 (30 Nov 2026), USENIX Security cycle 2 (26 Jan 2027), EUSIPCO (6 Feb 2027), Interspeech (9 Feb 2027), PoPETs Issue 4 (28 Feb 2027). The ICASSP satellite-workshop date (11 Nov 2026) has no confirmed workshop behind it yet.

1. **PoPETs 2027 Issue 3 (30 Nov 2026).** Best mix of fit and timing for a gradient-inversion privacy attack. Twelve pages plus unlimited appendices holds the closed-form derivation and three architectures, and the preprint policy is lenient. Notification on 1 Feb 2027 lands a week before the Interspeech deadline, so a rejection can be re-cut for Interspeech.
2. **Interspeech 2027 (9 Feb 2027).** Best audience fit for speech and federated ASR, and audio demos can go in the supplementary ZIP. The risk is space: if 2027 keeps the 2026 formats, a regular paper has 4 content pages and the long track has 8.
3. **USENIX Security '27 cycle 2 (26 Jan 2027).** Highest prestige with a realistic runway. The reviewer pool is security-first, so the paper would need a strong threat model and a defenses evaluation, plus the mandatory artifacts.

S&P cycle 2 is equal in prestige but six weeks out and carries the one-year resubmission lockout. ESANN is the soonest and easiest, but 6 pages including references is tight, prestige is lower, and publishing the core result there could block a full version elsewhere under the overlap rules.
